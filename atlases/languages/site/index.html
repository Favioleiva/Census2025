01 / Introduction

Data source and geographic coverage

2025 census

District tabulations from INEI REDATAM: 54 childhood-language variables, S5_P16_1–S5_P16_54.

National population aged 3+:
31,588,655

1,892 mapped units

The original 1,893-district table is preserved. In this derived atlas, Sangani is aggregated into Perené, retaining national totals.

Territorial reconciliation remains pending in 182 districts. The approved maps retain this source limitation.

Source: INEI, 2025 census; published language indicators.

02 / Reading the atlas

Two views of each census category

Absolute distribution

Number of persons reporting the category in each district: the category-1 count from the corresponding census variable.

Relative distribution

Percentage of the district population aged 3 years or older reporting that category.

100 × category count / population aged 3+

Fixed classes permit comparison across maps of the same indicator. Zero and missing values are distinct. Each language’s absolute map is immediately followed by its relative map.

Source: INEI, 2025 census; published language indicators.

06 / Methodological notes

A person can report more than one language

The 2025 results represent languages learned in childhood. INEI’s published results explicitly recognize people who learned more than one language.

The atlas uses 54 separate category counts. A person may contribute to more than one category, so adding these counts does not yield a count of unique persons.

The variables’ binary structure alone would not establish mutual exclusivity. The interpretation also reflects the census results and the validated totals.

Sources: INEI 2025 census results; local validated census tabulations.

06 / Methodological notes

Percentages need not sum to 100%

Every category percentage uses the same district population aged 3+ as its denominator. The numerators can overlap across categories.

Consequently, the percentages are category-specific reporting rates, not shares of a mutually exclusive partition.

The atlas preserves the validated percentages. It does not normalize them to sum to 100%, and the national ranking does not imply that categories are additive.

Source: INEI, 2025 census; published language indicators.

06 / Methodological notes

The population aged 3+ denominator

The denominator is obtained from the census age tabulations and reconciled with the universe of the original language exports in every district.

Children younger than 3 are excluded. The national denominator is 31,588,655 persons. GeoPackage population fields are not used to calculate the language percentages.

For Sangani and Perené, counts and age-eligible populations were added first; percentages were then recalculated from those totals. Percentages were not added.

Source: INEI, 2025 census; published language indicators.

06 / Methodological notes

Comparing 2025 with the 2017 census

Direct differences should not be interpreted as linguistic change until the question wording, response structure and category definitions have been reconciled.

Comparisons also require the same eligible age universe and an explicit correspondence between district boundaries. Matching district names alone is insufficient.

This presentation describes 2025. It does not present a harmonized 2017–2025 trend or infer gains and losses from unmatched categories.

Sources: INEI 2017 questionnaire; INEI 2025 questionnaire.

06 / Sources and coverage

Provenance and limits of interpretation

Statistical sources

INEI REDATAM language exports and age tabulations; published national ranking and district indicators.

Category labels are preserved from the query TXT files and Excel headers. The original official project dictionary was unavailable during validation.

Cartographic sources

Censo2025popFinalFixed.gpkg; 1,892 mapped units after aggregating Sangani into Perené. Original maps are reproduced unchanged.

All 182 district population discrepancies remain documented. Agreement of national totals does not establish district-level territorial equivalence.

Sources: INEI REDATAM; bundled map methodology and provenance records. The export footnote refers to territorial documentation as of 15 July 2026.
